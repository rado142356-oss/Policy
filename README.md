# Privacy Policy for My Discord Bot

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how My Discord Bot handles information when used on Discord.

## Information We Collect

My Discord Bot may process information provided by Discord when the bot is used, including:

* Discord user IDs
* Discord server IDs
* Discord channel IDs
* Discord message content, where required for the bot's functionality
* Commands and interactions sent to the bot

The bot does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

## How Information Is Used

Information processed by the bot is used only to:

* Provide the bot's features
* Respond to commands and interactions
* Maintain required bot functionality
* Store necessary configuration or server data

## Data Storage

If the bot stores information, it is stored only for the purposes necessary to provide its features. Data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

## Data Deletion

Users or server administrators may request deletion of data associated with the bot by contacting the bot owner.

## Third-Party Services

The bot operates through Discord and may use Discord's services and APIs. Discord's own privacy practices are governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change.

## Contact

For questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, contact the bot owner through the Discord server where the bot is operated.
